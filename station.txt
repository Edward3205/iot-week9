The broker's job in one sentence, and why the node never addresses the Pi
A: It receives every message that publishers and forwards each one to all the clients subscribed to that message's topic. 
The node sends its message to a topic, not to a specific machine. The broker receives the message and passes it to whoever subscribed to that topic. 

Your station's three topics, and what bench/+/light matches that bench/s07/ light does not
A: - Telemetry
   - Liveness
   - Commands
bench/s07/light matches only that one station's light topic. bench/+/light matches the light topic of every station.

QoS 0 verus QoS 1, and which this week's telemetry uses, with the reason.
A: it uses QoS , since a light reading is sent every second, so if one is lost, the next one arrives right away and fixes it.

Why config.py is git-ignored while config_example.py is commited.
A: config.py holds your real, private setings. If it were committed, those secrets would be stored in the repository's history,
and anyone who can see the repocould read them. Git-ignoring it keeps it only on your machine.
